Get Started in Under 60 Seconds

No complicated build scripts. Unzip and load straight into Google Chrome.

Download the Extension

Click the download button above to save code2linkedin-extension.zip and unzip it to any folder on your computer.

Extract: code2linkedin-extension.zip
